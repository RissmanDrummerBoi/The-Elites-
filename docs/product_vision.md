
# 🏠 Campus Residence Maintenance Reporting System (CRMRS)

## Introduction

The Campus Residence Maintenance Reporting System (CRMRS) is a centralized digital platform developed to improve how maintenance issues are reported and managed within university student residences at IWS.

The system creates a seamless communication channel between students, maintenance staff, and residence administrators by digitizing the reporting and tracking process.

---

# Problem Statement

Students living in campus residences frequently experience maintenance issues such as plumbing faults, electrical failures, broken furniture, and poor room conditions.

Currently, these issues are mostly reported verbally or through paperwork, which often results in delayed responses, poor communication, lack of accountability, and reduced student satisfaction.

For example, a student may experience an electrical fault late at night but struggle to find an efficient way to report the issue immediately, causing unnecessary delays and inconvenience.

This highlighted the need for a faster, more reliable, and more organized maintenance reporting system.

---

# Proposed Solution

Our proposed solution was to design the Campus Residence Maintenance Reporting System (CRMRS), a centralized digital platform that allows students to report maintenance issues directly from a web or mobile application.

The system automatically routes complaints to the appropriate maintenance department while allowing students to track issue progress in real time.

This improves communication, transparency, accountability, and response efficiency between students and maintenance teams.

---

# Unique Value Proposition

Unlike traditional verbal complaints and paper-based reporting systems, CRMRS offers:

* Real-time maintenance tracking
* Automated issue routing
* Instant notifications and updates
* Maintenance dashboards and analytics
* Centralized communication between students and maintenance departments

The platform improves operational efficiency while enhancing the overall student residence experience.

# User Workflow

1. Students register or log into the system using student credentials.

2. Students access the dashboard and select the maintenance category.

3. Students submit complaint details such as residence, room number, and issue description.

4. The system automatically routes the issue to the appropriate maintenance department.

5. Maintenance staff update the issue status from pending to resolved.

6. Students receive notifications and resolution confirmation.

---

# Success Metrics

To determine whether the system is successful, we identified measurable success metrics.

For example, if students previously waited almost a week to receive maintenance feedback, our goal is to reduce that response time to less than 24 hours.

We also aim to:

* Increase the number of resolved complaints
* Improve communication between students and maintenance staff
* Improve student satisfaction with residence maintenance services

These indicators help evaluate whether the system is effectively improving the maintenance reporting process.
